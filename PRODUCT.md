# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Technical recruiters, hiring managers, CTOs, and startup founders seeking a highly capable Junior Product Manager and AI Engineer who translates complex stakeholder requirements into scalable technical architectures.

## Product Purpose
To serve as an elite, high-fidelity interactive portfolio showcasing Anvith's career achievements, shipped systems, systems engineering mindset, and product management rigor (PRDs, ERDs, flows).

## Brand Personality
- Modernist
- Precise
- Expert confidence
- Sleek dark-steel, glassmorphic high-contrast aesthetic

## Anti-references
- Skeuomorphic consoles (retro dials, wood clipboards, leather binders, coffee stains, ruled notepad paper, screwheads).
- Saturated AI warm cream/sand/beige backgrounds.
- Over-rounded card borders (>16px).
- Tiny uppercase tracked eyebrows above every section.

## Design Principles
1. **Evidence over Assertion**: Support all claims with verified codebase evidence, schemas, and live operational metrics.
2. **Systems-Level Intuition**: Bridge product requirements with robust technical architecture, highlighting zero-cost infrastructures and performance wins.
3. **Precision Polish**: Ensure perfect vertical rhythm, crisp typography, and fluid spring-physics interactions.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- High contrast compliant body text (>= 4.5:1 ratio).
- Full `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` motion dampening.
- Accessible interactive drawer components with proper focus management.
